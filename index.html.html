The Mallana Story

Rooted in Village.
Raised with Values.

Three generations ago, our family began farming this land under the divine blessing of Lord Mallana Swamy — our village deity, our protector, our guiding light. Every acre is tended with the same reverence we offer in prayer.

For years, middlemen took the harvest of honest farmers and sold it to the cities — adding no value, only cost. We decided to break that chain. Why should a family pay ₹400 for something a farmer gets ₹60 for? It makes no sense.

Today, Mallana Farms is our family's answer — a direct bridge between our village soil and your city kitchen. You pay fair, our farmers earn fair, and the food you eat is the most honest it can ever be.
